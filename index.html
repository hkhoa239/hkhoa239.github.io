AAAI 2027 · Under review

HORSE — Geometry-Grounded Relational Reranking for Referring Expression Segmentation

Do Van Minh, Khoa Huynh, Zhengcheng Shen, Volodymyr Shcherbyna, Linh Kästner

Referring expression segmentation is hard when many similar instances appear and the target is specified by spatial, ordinal, comparative, or multi-object relations. Foundation models often produce a valid mask among candidates, yet confidence scoring still picks the wrong instance.

We introduce a lightweight geometry-grounded relational reranking adapter that treats this as candidate reranking over confidence-filtered SAM3 hypotheses: augment candidate tokens with normalized box geometry, model higher-order interactions via self-attention, condition on frozen CLIP text features, and fuse adapter scores with backbone confidence — without modifying or retraining the mask generator.

Improves SAM3 on all nine RefCOCO / RefCOCO+ / RefCOCOg / Ref-ZOM splits — e.g. RefCOCO+ TestB 43.68→54.64 (+10.96), RefCOCO+ Val +9.10, RefCOCO TestB +8.68 — with the largest gains on relation-intensive and same-category expressions.

ARENA Lab · In progress · Social navigation

BLINC — Identification-Driven Uncertainty for Social Robot Navigation

Teammates: Do Van Minh, Zhengcheng Shen, Khoa Huynh

Crowded navigation mixes two uncertainties: irreducible stochasticity of human motion (aleatoric) and ignorance of individual pedestrian styles (epistemic). Conventional stacks conflate them — overconfident around assertive people, or overly cautious (“freezing robot”).

BLINC separates offline learning of a behavior-conditioned world model from fast online identification of style latents. The pipeline runs on Arena-Rosnav (Arena 5.0) with HuNavSim under ROS 2, spawning pedestrians from parameter-randomized social-force models and logging behavioral parameters for supervised learning.

  • Phase 1 (current): Deterministic Behavior Encoder that predicts 5 normalized style parameters (desired speed, reaction distance, agility, path swing, predictability) via MSE regression on relative trajectory history.
  • Phase 2: Full Conditional VAE world-model decoder with stochastic latents for aleatoric uncertainty, plan-conditioned futures, NLL + KL (free-bits) + temporal consistency. Sim-to-real deferred.
