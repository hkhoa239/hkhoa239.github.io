Current projects

What I am working on.

VLA · In progress

MemoryPi

A memory-augmented vision–language–action policy. It stores and retrieves task-relevant information across subtasks, so an action can depend on an observation or outcome that is no longer in the current frame. The motivation is long-horizon failure in RoboMME-style manipulation, where models such as π0.5 mostly see the present or a short window.

Robot learning · In progress

Video-to-Simulation

A pipeline that recovers object geometry, hand–object interaction, and demonstrations from monocular human video, then retargets that interaction into a robot simulator for learning and evaluation. The aim is to start from naturally collected video rather than a hand-built scene.

Evaluation · In progress

Generative evaluation

Action-conditioned robot video and learned world models as a second evaluation signal, for scenes that are hard to reproduce with authored 3D assets. The question I am on now is when a predicted visual future is reliable enough to judge a policy, not only whether the video looks plausible.

Selected papers

Published and under review.

ICLR 2027 · Under review

Reliable Few-Point Metric Grounding for Monocular Depth Foundation Models

Monocular depth foundation models are strong and metrically unanchored. This work asks how few sparse metric observations are enough to ground them, and how that grounding holds when some observations are wrong.

On NYUv2 with UniDepthV2, 8 observations reduce AbsRel from 0.0766 to 0.0540 (29.5%). When 80% of observations are corrupted, AbsRel remains 0.0608.

Image and Computer Vision · Under review

Acquisition-Aware Anomaly Detection

Anomaly detectors often treat blur, motion, illumination, and sensor noise as defects. This work introduces the ARAD benchmark for those acquisition shifts, and a training-free framework that separates a change in how the scene was captured from evidence of a real defect.

ICRA 2027 · Under review

Mutual Hand–Object Reasoning for Egocentric Manipulation Perception

Egocentric manipulation perception usually reasons from the hand to the object. This work lets hand and object evidence refine each other.

On the EgoHOS out-of-distribution split, the method improves 6.93 mIoU over CaRe-Ego, and 3.82 mIoU on mini-HOI4D in a cross-dataset setting without fine-tuning.
